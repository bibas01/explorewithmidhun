Every year, I watch trekkers struggle up the Namche hill carrying 20kg backpacks filled with things they will never use. 

Here is the truth: you don't need half of what the internet tells you to bring.

### The Big Three

You should spend your money here. Don't cheap out on these.

1. **Boots:** They must be broken in. Do not buy new boots the week before your trek.
2. **Sleeping Bag:** A down bag rated to -10°C. Teahouses are cold, and their blankets are thin.
3. **Down Jacket:** You need a serious, puffy down jacket for the mornings and evenings above Dingboche.

### What to Leave at Home

- **Cotton Jeans:** They get wet, take days to dry, and offer zero insulation. Wear trekking pants.
- **A Drone:** The Nepali government requires expensive permits to fly drones in national parks, and locals find them annoying.
- **Heavy Camera Gear:** Unless you are a professional photographer, your phone takes great photos. Enjoy the view with your eyes.
